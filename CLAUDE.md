# Member App V1 Reference Status

This repository is the visual and behavior reference for the shell-first Member
App rebuild. It is not the target workspace for the new implementation.

- Active roadmap: [`../ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md).
- Preserve V1 as reference/fallback.
- Do not copy direct Firestore access, business logic, credentials, or legacy
  services into the new app.
- Approved UI, assets, navigation, and interaction behavior may be extracted
  only through the roadmap review gates.
